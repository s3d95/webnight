Privacy Policy

How we handle your data when you use our bots.

1. What We Collect

We only collect what we need to run the bots for you:

  • Your Discord server and user IDs, so the bot knows where it's working
  • Anything you send us through support
  • Basic usage info, like which commands get used

2. How We Use It

We use this information to run and improve the service:

  • To set up and manage your subscription
  • To keep the bots fast and online
  • To answer your support questions
  • To meet our legal and security obligations

3. Sharing Your Data

We don't sell or trade your data. We only share it with the trusted services we use to keep the bots running, or when we're legally required to — for example, by Discord or by law.

4. Keeping It Safe

We use standard encryption to protect your data, and access is limited to the people who actually run the service.

5. How Long We Keep It

We only keep your data as long as we need it. Payment records are kept as required by law, and routine logs (like command errors) are deleted within 14–30 days.

6. Message Content

When a server admin sets up a music request channel with /setup, our music bot reads the text of messages sent in that single channel only, so it can find and play the song you typed. These messages are processed instantly and then deleted.

  • We do not store message content — it is used only to play your request, then discarded.
  • We never read messages in any other channel.
  • We never use message content to train AI or machine-learning models, and we do not sell or share it.

7. Your Choices

You can ask us to delete your data at any time — just open a ticket in our Discord support server.
